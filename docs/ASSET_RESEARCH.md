## 2. Recommendation: draw both backgrounds in code (no outside assets)
Because the picture sits at a whole-number scale in a window of any shape (16:9 ... 32:9), a fixed picture frame cannot fit; a drawn one fits every size exactly, adds no files to ship, and has no licence question (it is our own work, released with the project).
* **Apple II CRT Monitor Bezel:** an Apple Monitor II / III style housing drawn around the picture: platinum-beige plastic with a bevelled edge and a faint grain, a recessed dark inner frame, rounded glass corners hugging the picture exactly, a small power light and a ventilation grille at the bottom. No Apple logo or lettering (trademarks). Nothing is drawn over the picture.
* **Apple II Desktop:** an understated GS/OS-style desktop: a soft grey-blue dither pattern, a thin menu bar with plain menu words (no Apple logo), and the game in a window with a title bar ("PAC-MAN") and two small boxes, set in the Apple II character font. No clutter; the game stays the centre.
* Both follow the picture's size and position at every resolution and display mode and are compared against the picture in tests (the picture area is never touched).
* **Alternative if you prefer a photograph:** I would check one candidate's Commons file page (author, licence text, resolution) and ask you before downloading it; a photo can only be a fixed-size backdrop behind the picture (not a frame that fits), and needs the credit line in the release notes.
* **Alternative that needs nothing from me:** the program could also load your own PNG backgrounds from a folder (not built; nothing of yours would be shipped).
